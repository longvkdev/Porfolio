TAPE MONKE

“If it moves, tape it.”

A single-player Quest 3 game for the studio's 3-day “Make the world your playground” jam. You're a monke with an infinite roll of tape: grab any prop, slap it onto anything and it sticks and becomes climbable — so every build is new terrain.

  • Gorilla-arm locomotion, physics hands and a full-body IK avatar; tape and peel with cascade collapses, every strip in one GPU-instanced draw call
  • Studio Defense mode: gremlin waves, a 160-agent instanced horde, King Gremlin boss, AI crew fighters and first-person cinematics
  • Level, lighting and an original rigged Blender art kit generated from code; interactive voice tutorial and day/night cycle
  • Built headless (Unity batch mode + adb) with an on-device “chaos bot” logging performance on every build

SHIFT//01 — Fulfillment Protocol

“Pick, pack, seal, scan, load — against the clock.”

A playable first-person warehouse prototype built as a demo for an Upwork job. The first playable came together in about a day, then physical handling, hand poses and replay were refined through targeted tests.

  • 65 physical goods and cartons with mass, friction and collision; procedural IK and finger poses for grabbing, rotating and side-gripping big boxes
  • JSON-defined tasks drive orders, timers, scoring, a local leaderboard and step-by-step guidance
  • Records inputs, camera and 81 tracked entities at 60 Hz; replay with seeking, an event timeline and integrity validation
